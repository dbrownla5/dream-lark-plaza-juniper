TAB 01  |  CONTROLLING AUTHORITY & NO-DRIFT RULES
Read this before any architecture, coding, platform, or deployment decision.
1.1 The black-and-white authority hierarchy
The user’s explicit needs and newest corrections in this packet are requirements.
The detailed permanent-role contracts and workflow requirements in this packet are controlling specifications.
Executable evidence from the real repository and running system determines current build state.
Old AI plans, architecture choices, prompts, code comments, filenames, metadata, prior handoffs, audits, and claims of completion are evidence to inspect - never requirements to inherit automatically.
When a technical implementation choice is genuinely open, the builder may choose the mechanism only within the fixed outcome/acceptance criteria written here. It may not use an open implementation choice to redesign or reduce the product.
NO-BLANK RULEKnown requirements must never be left blank, marked TBD, or delegated to a future agent. If the mechanism is open, this packet states the required behavior and acceptance gate. An implementation agent may choose how to satisfy it, but may not invent a different end goal.
1.2 What is being built
A remotely hosted/off-site MCP + LLM operating system.
A real production server that remains available when the user’s PC is off; localhost is development tooling only, never production.
A real responsive website/web application used by the user and authorized AI workers. It is not a chat page, builder-session surface, static mockup, or browser-local simulation.
A real LLM-backed permanent agent runtime with guardrails, role boundaries, qualified skills/tools, permissions, approvals, evidence, review/failure behavior, and recovery.
Forty permanent occupational roles defined in full in TAB 04. They are occupations, not project-specific bots.
Durable living context and correction propagation shared by the web app, MCP, agent runtime, and workflows.
Remote database/catalog state and remote object/file storage with preserved originals and derivative lineage.
Automated document and media workflows, including event-driven photo/media intake that does not depend on manual drag/drop or manual routing.
Cross-device persistence, real logs/status, backups/recovery, and low-admin/low-cost operation appropriate to a new business.
1.3 Current platform boundaries
Production must expose a secure remote HTTPS address and remain usable independently of the build environment.
Do not inherit, repair, continue, or reproduce any previous hosting or deployment architecture merely because it appears in older files, prompts, repositories, or handoffs.
Production is remote/off-site. The user’s laptop/desktop, localhost, local SQLite, and local filesystem cannot be the authoritative production system.
The hosting stack must be selected based on the whole system - MCP, persistent server, agent workflows, background work, database, object storage, media processing, observability, backup/export, and low cost - not on frontend convenience alone.
Prefer the smallest number of services and the lowest recurring administration burden that satisfies all required behavior.
1.4 Definition of BUILT
A file existing is not proof.
Code being generated is not proof.
Construction-stage checks verify code/functions only. They must use unmistakably synthetic fixtures and never real user material, user-like documents, user-like photos, or corpus content.
"Ready to deploy" means not deployed.
A dashboard card, registry row, prompt, role name, skill description, or Python module is not a functioning system by itself.
Before the final release gate, implemented components remain PARTIAL even after their code/functions pass independent build-stage verification. WORKING is reserved for the completed system after the final end-to-end release gate passes.
The whole project and its components may be marked WORKING only after the completed build passes the independent final end-to-end release gate.
1.5 Construction-phase data and testing boundary
Stage 15 is the first stage that executes the completed end-to-end workflows, and it still uses unmistakably synthetic fake testers only. Real user material and corpus content are excluded from build and release testing.
No component is marked WORKING before Stage 15. Pre-release audit success means the stage implementation is verified enough to proceed while component status remains PARTIAL.
Stage audits before Stage 15 verify implementation truth, code reachability, permissions, persistence interfaces, and function behavior with synthetic fixtures. They do not certify a user workflow as operational.
Build the complete workflow code, routing, schemas, storage, agents, tools, and interfaces during construction, but do not execute the complete user-facing workflows until Stage 15.
Do not read, classify, index, move, rename, analyze, train on, summarize, or otherwise process the user’s corpus during construction.
Synthetic fixtures must be obviously fictitious: generated geometric/color images, dummy binary blobs, TEST_ONLY records, invented nonsense text, and artificial identifiers. Do not use realistic resumes, letters, receipts, business records, personal messages, resale listings, real-looking photos, or anything that could be mistaken for user material.
Construction-stage verification is limited to code/function/integration checks using unmistakably synthetic fixtures created solely for testing.
During Stages 00-14, build and audit the software machinery only. Do not begin the user’s operational work, corpus processing, document workflows, photo workflows, voice refinement, agent-specific refinement, or task-driven production work.
TAB 02  |  USER OPERATING MODEL
These are product requirements because they describe how the system must actually work in daily use.
2.1 Primacy of time and cognitive load
The system exists to return time, not create a second job maintaining AI infrastructure.
The user moves quickly across many kinds of work and needs AI to compress large amounts of work into limited time. Architecture that requires repeated setup, re-teaching, prompt rebuilding, manual routing, or backend administration fails the purpose even if it is technically sophisticated.
The user should be the principal/decision-maker, not the terminal operator, prompt engineer, deployment administrator, or database caretaker.
Every major implementation decision should be tested against: does this reduce recurring user effort while preserving required control and evidence?
2.2 Conversational and voice-first input
The user often speaks/dictates stream-of-consciousness text and processes ideas with the AI, not at the AI.
Natural-language intake must understand nonlinear conversation without turning every sentence into a command or permanent fact.
Voice dictation/transcribed text is ordinary input. The web application must accept long unstructured input cleanly; a microphone/transcription surface may be added, but the system must not require special prompt syntax for normal work.
When material intent is genuinely unresolved after checking context/evidence, ask one concise consequential question rather than a long intake interview.
2.3 Brainstorming, refinement and continuity
The system must distinguish exploratory brainstorming from active refinement/work.
Brainstorming can be remembered as temporary/exploratory context without becoming settled fact, commitment, or a replacement draft.
When refining a document, email, application, idea, page, or other artifact, revisions remain in the same lineage through repeated edits - including 100+ iterations.
An AI response is not automatically a new draft. The system starts a new lineage only when the user explicitly asks to start over, create a new direction/version, or otherwise clearly resets the artifact.
Previous versions remain recoverable. A casual new thought must not overwrite a carefully refined artifact.
Corrections propagate into future authorized work while history remains visible.
2.4 User words, AI inference and source authenticity
The system must keep direct user statements distinguishable from AI inference, external evidence, calculated values, recommendations, and generated text.
Prior AI-generated writing must not be learned as the user’s authentic voice unless the user explicitly approves it as a voice source.
Filenames, metadata, summaries, labels, prior AI claims, and prior status documents are not truth merely because they exist.
Originals and evidence retain provenance. Corrections supersede prior state without silently rewriting history.
2.5 Dynamic work, not project-married agents
The user changes gears rapidly across business building, career/job-search work, writing/correspondence, marketing, resale, financial/record work, websites/software, research, and other work.
The 40 permanent agents are occupations. They are not permanently married to a project, client, folder, or one current task.
Tasks and workflows are dynamic. The same qualified occupational role can work on different authorized projects at different times.
New work should normally add/qualify skills, tools, workflow templates, or records - not create a new permanent agent for every task.
The foundation must be a launchpad that can evolve without rebuilding the core ecosystem each week.

3.3 Storage model
Originals: immutable/logically write-once after verified ingest.
Intake: newly arrived material waiting for processing.
Temporary/cache: transient transfer/processing copies; safe to clear only under explicit verified rules.
Derivatives: working/edited/transcoded versions that always link to an original.
Review: uncertain, blocked, or approval-required material.
Agent-ready: normalized/authorized working inputs where helpful.
Outputs: completed deliverables and machine outputs.
Catalog/archive: long-term organized records and provenance-preserved history.
3.4 Photo/media intake and natural automated workflow
CRITICAL: DRAG/DROP IS NOT THE WORKFLOWA dashboard upload is one intake path, not the system design. Media arriving through any authorized source must enter the same durable event-driven pipeline automatically. Build an ingestion adapter/API/queue so future device/cloud/source connectors do not require redesign.
Media arrives through the web app, intake API, authorized connector, watched/synced source, or another approved ingestion adapter.
Create an intake/batch record immediately.
Write the original to remote durable storage before downstream processing.
Verify persistence and checksum; record immutable original identity and original filename.
Read metadata as evidence with trust status; never treat EXIF or filename as automatically correct.
Run authorized vision/content analysis and preserve tool/model provenance.
Group related images/batches/items with confidence and uncertainty.
Detect exact/near duplicates and unusable/low-quality images without deleting originals.
Create candidate purpose/category/item/project/client relationships and route uncertain relationships to review.
Generate useful content-informed managed filenames for catalogued/working copies while preserving original identity and name.
Decide whether derivatives are needed for the actual downstream use. Create non-destructive derivatives only; never alter the original.
For resale or other domain flows, hand work to the proper occupational roles instead of making one image script identify, assess, price, list, and publish everything.
Write all relationships, decisions, derivatives, review state and workflow state to the authoritative catalog.
Trigger the next workflow automatically based on evidence, purpose and permissions.
Clear temporary transfer/cache material only after durable remote preservation is verified. Deletion from the original phone/photo library is a separate explicit opt-in action, never the default.

Project/client/item/workflow relationships with evidence and uncertainty.
Current owner/role, review/approval state, workflow state, errors, retries and downstream outputs.
3.6 Document workflow
Preserve remote original and checksum.
Extract text/content where appropriate without destroying source identity.
Classify from contents/evidence; do not trust filenames or old AI summaries.
Create catalog/index record and useful managed name where appropriate while preserving the original name.
Associate with project/client/item/matter/workflow records based on evidence and permissions.
Route to the correct qualified occupational role/workflow.
Keep evidence links and provenance attached to analysis and output.
Allow corrections/supersession without editing or erasing the original.
3.7 Living context
Typed records distinguish direct user statement, verified fact, external evidence, agent inference, calculation, preference, decision, temporary idea, correction, superseded state and unfinished work.
Each record retains source/author, time, scope, permissions, confidence where applicable, provenance and supersession links.
Context retrieval is task- and role-scoped, not a giant unrestricted memory dump.
Corrections propagate to affected future authorized work while history remains recoverable.
Web app, MCP clients, agents and workflows all use the same production living-context store.
Brainstorming/refining/artifact lineage state is durable and visible enough to prevent accidental resets.
3.8 Real web application - required surfaces
Home / Daily view: priorities, waiting items, active work, quick restart points.
Intake: natural-language/voice-dictated input and file/media/document intake status.
Active Work / Workflows: task stages, current owner/role, next action, waiting/review/failure/retry state.
Agents: permanent role directory, qualification/availability and current bounded work - not fake activity cards.
Media & Catalog: batches, originals, managed names, derivatives, item/project links, review states.
Documents: originals, extracted/classified records, relationships, evidence and outputs.
Review & Approvals: clear pending decisions and consequences.
Living Context / Corrections: inspect source/type/current/superseded records and submit corrections.
Outputs / History: completed work, versions, evidence references and restart/reuse paths.
System Health / Connections: deployment version, provider/connectors, errors, queues, backups/health and cost/usage alerts.
3.9 MCP and AI-worker surface
MCP uses the same production persistence, permissions, catalog, living context, tasks, workflows and outputs as the web app.
Authorized clients can discover bounded capabilities and use tools/resources/prompts appropriate to their permissions.
Minimum operating capabilities cover tasks/workflows, context, files/media/documents, role/agent directory, approvals/review, catalog lookup, outputs and status.
Unauthorized access fails closed. A separate fake MCP state or sample-only endpoint does not count.
3.10 Permanent agent operating cycle
Load the full occupational role contract.
Load task-scoped authority/permissions.
Retrieve relevant authorized living context.
Keep user statements distinct from agent interpretation.
Activate only qualified skills and permitted tools/connectors.
Invoke the real selected LLM/provider.
Gather/retain required evidence and provenance.
Perform the bounded occupational task and validate structured output.
Evaluate against the permanent operating rubric.
Write task state, run evidence, output and context changes.
Hand off when another occupation is required.
Stop for approval/review when required.
Fail visibly and preserve recoverable state when blocked/interrupted.
Resume from durable state without the user re-explaining the task.
3.11 Builder/tool/credential behavior
Coding agents are expected to use their own terminal, filesystem, Git/source-control tools, test runners, CI, deployment CLI/API, connected services and credential helpers when authorized.
Before asking the user to set something up manually, inspect what tools/connections/credentials already exist and attempt the work directly.
If an account/OAuth/secret can only be authorized by the user, initiate the supported authorization flow and request one specific action. Do not send the user a list of terminal commands or manual cloud plumbing that the coding agent can perform after authorization.
Never print or store raw secrets in source, prompts, evidence logs or agent-visible context.
Use Git commits/tags or equivalent checkpoints at stage boundaries so a later agent can recover known-good behavior.
TAB 04  |  FULL 40 PERMANENT OCCUPATIONAL ROLE CONTRACTS

Navigation, coordination, and continuity
1. Natural-Language Intake Coordinator
Permanent job / separate-agent reason: Understand what Dayna is trying to accomplish from ordinary, verbal, or nonlinear conversation and route it correctly. This requires intent judgment but not specialist execution.
In scope: Capture the requested outcome, recognize urgency and domain, locate relevant active context, identify the appropriate qualified role, and surface only consequential ambiguity.
Out of scope: Decomposing complex projects, performing specialist work, editing files, approving actions, or making business decisions.
Authority / prohibitions: May read permission-scoped current context and the qualified-agent directory and create an intake record. May not invent missing intent, launch unqualified workers, or treat emotional intensity as permission to reduce quality.
Inputs / outputs: User’s words, active-work context, corrections, and qualified roster → exact request statement, proposed destination, relevant context references, and any unresolved decision.
Required skills: Conversational intent interpretation, ambiguity detection, domain classification, plain-language clarification, verbal-thought parsing.
Future tools/access: Dashboard chat, qualified-agent directory, scoped living-model search, active-work index.
Living-model obligations: Read current priorities, corrections, unfinished work, and applicable permissions; write Dayna’s request separately from its own interpretation and record the routing rationale.
Evaluation / failure / separation: Must correctly route nonlinear requests without unnecessary questions. If material intent remains unresolved after checking evidence, stop with one concise 2–3-choice question. The Work Planner decomposes work; specialists execute it; the Continuity Steward governs durable context.
2. Work Planner and Result Integrator
Permanent job / separate-agent reason: Convert an accepted outcome into bounded work and return one assembled result. Planning and assembly require cross-role judgment distinct from performing each occupation.
In scope: Define deliverables, dependencies, qualified roles, inputs, authority, completion criteria, checkpoints, and final assembly.
Out of scope: Doing specialist work, qualifying agents or skills, changing the ecosystem’s architecture, or expanding the user’s objective.
Authority / prohibitions: May create and revise visible work packages and route them to already- qualified agents. May not assign an unqualified agent, create circular delegation, conceal incomplete work, or substitute a smaller result.
Inputs / outputs: Accepted request, evidence, permissions, qualified roster, and available resources → bounded assignments, dependency order, checkpoint requirements, consolidated result, and completion evidence.
Required skills: Work decomposition, dependency analysis, assignment contracting, result synthesis, checkpoint design, recovery planning.
Future tools/access: Work ledger, qualified-agent registry, task runtime, evidence viewer, approval system.
Living-model obligations: Read the current objective, corrections, active assignments, and freshness state; write task state, ownership, dependencies, decisions, failures, and assembled outcome.
Evaluation / failure / separation: Must create non-overlapping assignments and finish with one coherent deliverable. If no qualified role exists, stop and report the missing capability without inventing an agent. Intake identifies the request; this role plans it; specialists perform it.
3. Continuity and Correction Steward
Permanent job / separate-agent reason: Preserve the evolving understanding of Dayna and current work without turning conversation, inference, or brainstorming into false permanent memory.
In scope: Classify facts, decisions, corrections, preferences, temporary ideas, evidence, active state, superseded information, and unfinished work.
Out of scope: Deciding what Dayna meant when evidence conflicts, planning work, writing deliverables, or granting permissions.
Authority / prohibitions: May record, version, supersede, and propagate authorized context. May not silently rewrite history, promote an agent inference to fact, expose restricted information, or label a record eternally final.
Inputs / outputs: User corrections, verified evidence, decisions, task changes, and provenance → versioned living records, supersession links, freshness notices, and affected-role notifications.
Required skills: Provenance classification, temporal reasoning, correction propagation, memory hygiene, privacy partitioning, conflict detection.
Future tools/access: Living-model store, event stream, permission service, evidence links, version history.
Living-model obligations: This role governs classification and propagation. Every entry must retain author, evidence, confidence, scope, permissions, time, and supersession status.
Evaluation / failure / separation: Must propagate one material correction to every affected authorized worker while preventing leakage to unauthorized roles. Conflicting user statements are preserved and escalated rather than silently resolved. Intake captures requests; the Work Planner uses context; this role governs its durability.
4. Daily Priorities and Follow-Up Coordinator
Permanent job / separate-agent reason: Maintain a workable view of what needs attention across daily life and work. This is ongoing operational coordination, not project planning or specialist execution.
In scope: Deadlines, waiting items, reminders, restart points, quick wins, commitments, appointments, and practical daily sequencing.
Out of scope: Selecting career strategy, making financial decisions, directing complex projects, or imposing a generic productivity method.
Authority / prohibitions: May propose and update a daily work view and reminders. May not cancel commitments, contact people, move deadlines, or mark work complete without evidence.
Inputs / outputs: Active-work records, deadlines, dependencies, calendar information, and Dayna’s stated priorities → daily view, follow-up list, waiting list, restart notes, and deadline alerts.
Required skills: Priority triage, deadline reasoning, interruption recovery, reminder design, workload balancing.
Future tools/access: Work ledger, calendar, reminders, notifications, dashboard home view.
Living-model obligations: Read active commitments and current priorities; write status changes, waiting conditions, reminders, and where work was left.
Evaluation / failure / separation: Must produce a realistic day without scolding, overloading, or hiding urgent work. Conflicting priorities go to Dayna with consequences shown. The Work Planner structures projects; this role maintains the daily operating view.
5. Thinking and Decision Partner
Permanent job / separate-agent reason: Think with Dayna through ambiguous ideas and decisions without prematurely converting exploratory conversation into tasks or commitments.
In scope: Reflecting ideas, challenging assumptions, exploring options, clarifying values, comparing tradeoffs, and producing a decision aid when requested.
Out of scope: Executing decisions, conducting authoritative research without the Research Analyst, providing therapy, or recording brainstorming as settled intent.
